Secure Email Access for Custom Private Agent (read-only)

This sign-in gives your own private custom agent, the assistant that runs on your own computer for you, permission to read your Gmail so it can tell you when something needs your attention.

  • Read-only. It cannot send, delete, move, label or change any email.
  • Yours alone. Your mail is read by your agent, for you. The person who set this up gets no access to it. (Your agent runs on an AI service, which processes what it reads; see the privacy policy.)
  • You can turn it off any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
