Privacy Policy

Secure Email Access for Custom Private Agent (read-only). Last updated November 18, 2026.

What this is

This sign-in lets your own private custom agent, an assistant running on your own computer, read your Gmail so it can let you know about messages that matter to you.

What it can access

Read-only access to your Gmail messages and settings (Google's gmail.readonly permission). It cannot send, delete, move, label or change anything.

How your data is used

Your email is read only by your agent, on your computer, to decide what to tell you about. It is used for nothing else.

Who can see it

Your email is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shown to anyone else, including the person who created this sign-in. Your agent runs on an AI service (for example Claude); when it reads a message to decide what to tell you, that message is processed by the AI service under that service's own privacy terms, the same as anything else you ask your agent to read. A first sorting step that sets aside obvious junk runs on your own computer.

Where it is stored

Any notes your agent keeps about your email stay on your own computer.

Turning it off

You can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Changing your Google password also ends it.

Google's rules

This sign-in's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
